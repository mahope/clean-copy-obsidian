What the three types mean

  • Protanopia — reduced sensitivity to red light. Reds look darker and can vanish against green or grey.
  • Deuteranopia — reduced sensitivity to green light. The most common form; red and green become nearly indistinguishable.
  • Tritanopia — reduced sensitivity to blue light. Very rare (~0.01%); blue and yellow merge together.

Practical rule: never rely on hue alone to convey meaning. Pair color with icons, labels or patterns — that is also what WCAG SC 1.4.1 requires.
